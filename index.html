Francine Stephens

Data Scientist | Product Analytics, Experimentation & Behavioral Insights

I use data to understand how people experience products, uncover what drives their behavior, and turn evidence into better decisions.

I’m a data scientist and product analytics practitioner who works at the intersection of experimentation, behavioral analytics, measurement, and research. I combine quantitative methods with product context to answer questions like: What are people doing? Why are they doing it? How should we measure it? And what should we do differently as a result?

At Workday, I support product, design, and UX teams through experimentation, behavioral measurement, AI/agent evaluation, segmentation, and predictive modeling. Previously, at Mozilla, I combined product analytics, experimentation, surveys, and unstructured user feedback to understand the Firefox experience. At Carmel Partners, I applied causal and spatial methods to questions about markets, investment strategy, and the built environment.

Across these settings, my focus is consistent: finding signal in complex behavioral data and making it useful for people who have to make decisions.
