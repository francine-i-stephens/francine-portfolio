I’m a behavioral data scientist who has used causal inference, experimentation, and spatial analysis to understand what drives human behavior — and to help teams design better products, policies, and systems.

My work blends rigorous quantitative methods with engaging, human-centered storytelling. Whether the “place” is a digital interface or a physical neighborhood, I’m interested in how design, incentives, and environment shape human choices — and how evidence can guide better decisions.
