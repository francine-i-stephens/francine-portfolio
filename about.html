About Me

I’m a behavioral data scientist who has experience working in the areas of product design, human behavior, and real estate. Across both digital and physical environments, I study how people navigate the spaces they inhabit — candidate dashboards, neighborhood streets, rental markets, and emerging AI interfaces — and how design and incentives shape their decisions.

What I Focus On

  • Product experimentation and behavioral metrics
  • Causal inference and econometric modeling
  • Urban policy, housing dynamics, and spatial analysis
  • AI-assisted analysis and explainability research
  • Data storytelling for product, policy, and communications teams

My Background

My work spans product experimentation at Workday, market and neighborhood analysis in real estate investment, and academic research on Airbnb markets using NLP and spatial methods. I’m drawn to questions about how environments — digital or physical — shape human behavior, and how evidence can guide better design and policy.

How I Work

My approach blends rigorous quantitative methods with clear, human-centered storytelling. I believe the best insights come from pairing causal inference and behavioral evidence with narratives that help teams understand not just what happened, but why it matters — and what to do next.
