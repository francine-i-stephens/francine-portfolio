About Me

I’m a statistician and experimentation specialist who builds behavioral metrics frameworks, modernizes experimentation reporting, and helps product teams make confident, evidence‑based decisions. My work blends rigorous causal inference with clear, human‑centered storytelling — because data only matters when people can understand it, trust it, and act on it.

What I Focus On

  • Designing and analyzing A/B experiments in pre‑production and production environments
  • Building behavioral metrics that reveal friction, intent, and hidden user patterns
  • Creating automated analysis pipelines that improve consistency and reduce time‑to‑insight
  • Translating complex statistical findings into clear, actionable narratives
  • Exploring neighborhood change, urban design, and causal inference in real‑world settings

My Background

Before working in experimentation, I was a data scientist in real estate investment, where I built demographic and submarket analyses to guide portfolio strategy. I’ve also published academic work using NLP to analyze Airbnb data, and I continue to explore how urban design and policy shape neighborhood dynamics.

How I Work

My approach is grounded in clarity, precision, and empathy. I believe the best insights come from pairing rigorous statistical methods with thoughtful communication — helping teams see not just what happened, but why it matters.
